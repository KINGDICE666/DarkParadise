space_crawl
/obj/effect/proc_holder/spell/jaunt/space_crawl

Lets the caster enter and exit tiles of space or misc turfs.

Vars

invalid_turf_messageMessage shown when the caster tries to enter/exit on a turf that isn't valid (see is_valid_turf).
jaunt_hand_typeThe "hands" given to a carbon jaunter to stop them acting. Subtypes override this to rename them.
jaunt_in_soundSound played at the turf when the caster submerges into the jaunt. Subtypes override for their own flavour.
jaunt_out_soundSound played at the turf when the caster resurfaces from the jaunt. Subtypes override for their own flavour.
jaunting_traitsList of traits that are added to the heretic while in space phase jaunt

Procs

try_exit_jauntAttempts to Exit the passed space or misc turf.
